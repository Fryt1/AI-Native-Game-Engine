# Stage Kind: Investigation

Use for diagnosis, investigation, comparison, inspection, and uncertainty
reduction.

## Execution checklist knowledge

- define the question, scope, and current uncertainty
- identify objects and evidence sources
- collect reproducibility and environment facts
- record candidate explanations separately from facts
- select the next observation that best distinguishes candidates

## Acceptance checklist knowledge

- required environment, version, file, and configuration facts were checked
- reproducibility is known
- major candidates were tested or explicitly left unknown
- every conclusion references evidence
- unsupported conclusions remain `unknown`
- the declared stop condition is met

Evidence-gathering items may expand as new facts arrive. Failure to prove a root
cause is not failure when the Stage correctly reports the remaining unknowns.
