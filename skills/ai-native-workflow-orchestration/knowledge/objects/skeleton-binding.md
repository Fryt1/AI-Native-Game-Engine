# Object Knowledge: Skeleton and Binding

## Before execution

Inspect bone names and count, parent hierarchy, Skeleton reference, bind pose,
skin weights, Physics Asset policy, and whether structural bone changes are
allowed.

## After execution

Check bone names/count, parent hierarchy, bind pose, skin-weight coverage,
Skeleton and Physics Asset references, and forbidden hierarchy changes.

## Evidence

Use structured source/target skeleton inventories and a comparison Tool for
complex hierarchy or weight validation.
